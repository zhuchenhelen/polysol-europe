Products

We supply PP, PE and PVC across common grades for injection molding, film, pipe and profile applications. Every grade is listed with its origin so you can assess tariff and logistics implications upfront.

PP

Polypropylene

Homopolymer & copolymer. Injection, raffia, film and fiber grades. Typical grades: T30S, K8003, EPS30R.

PE

Polyethylene

HDPE, LDPE and LLDPE. Injection, film, pipe and blow molding grades. Typical grades: 5000S, 2426H, 7042.

PVC

Polyvinyl Chloride

Calcium carbide and ethylene route. SG-5, SG-8 and equivalent. Pipe, profile and flexible grades.

Multi-Region Sourcing

European plastic processors face increasing uncertainty — from anti-dumping duties on certain origins to freight volatility and supply disruptions. Polysol Europe addresses this by sourcing across three regions.

United States

Competitive pricing on select PE and PP grades, with improving tariff conditions for EU imports.

South Korea

Established quality for PP and PE, with a well-developed export infrastructure to Europe.

China

Broad grade availability and flexible volume — suitable for both spot and repeat orders.

Tariff, Anti-Dumping & REACH Support

We provide transparent compliance information for every origin we supply — current duty rates, anti-dumping risk flags, and REACH documentation on request.

We do not guarantee "no tariff." Instead, we give you the current status and help you plan accordingly.
