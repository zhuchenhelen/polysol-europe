Products

We supply a full range of polymer resins for European manufacturers. Grades and specifications are aligned with the supply capabilities of KPCC and Polymer Chemical Corp.

PP

Polypropylene

Homopolymer & copolymer. Injection, raffia, film and fiber grades.

PE

Polyethylene

HDPE, LDPE and LLDPE. Injection, film, pipe and blow molding grades.

PVC

Polyvinyl Chloride

Calcium carbide and ethylene route. Pipe, profile and flexible grades.

PET

Polyethylene Terephthalate

Bottle grade, fiber grade and film grade. For packaging and textile applications.

PS

Polystyrene

GPPS and HIPS. Injection and extrusion grades for packaging and consumer goods.

Supply Chain

Polysol Europe is the European sales company of KPCC and Polymer Chemical Corp. Supply for European customers is arranged through our sister companies, who procure directly from producers in the United States and Asia.

United States

PE, PP and PET grades arranged through Polymer Chemical Corp's established relationships with US producers.

South Korea

PP and PE arranged through KPCC, with well-developed export infrastructure to European markets.

China

Broad grade availability and flexible volume — suitable for both spot and repeat orders.

About Polysol Europe

Polysol Europe Sp. z o.o. is the European sales company of KPCC and Polymer Chemical Corp. We serve European manufacturers with PP, PE, PVC, PET and PS raw materials, with supply arranged through our sister companies' established procurement network.
